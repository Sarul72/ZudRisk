# ZudRisk v0.2 (NASA snow and pasture) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the two missing hazards: snow depth from NASA GLDAS-2.1 and summer pasture from MODIS NDVI. Score six winters with all four indicators, validate each against NSO losses plus a pooled test, and let the site switch between winters.

**Architecture:**
- Two new source modules (`gldas.py`, `ndvi.py`) fetch Mongolia-only subsets over NASA's Hyrax OPeNDAP, using the Earthdata login already in `~/.netrc`.
- Each reduces its grid to one value per soum and month (`soum, yyyymm, value`). `indicators.py` turns those into z-scores exactly as it does for cold.
- A new `hindcast.py` runs indicators, validate and export over the winter list, then writes the pooled result.
- `nso.py` also keeps aimag rows, because NSO has no soum-level losses for 2010.

**Tech Stack:**
- v0.1's stack, plus `netCDF4`. It was approved on 2026-11-08 and is already in `pyproject.toml` / `uv.lock`, uncommitted.
- Downloads use stdlib `urllib` with a cookie jar and `~/.netrc`. No `earthaccess`, `xarray` or `rasterio`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-zudrisk-design.md`. Previous plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-08-zudrisk-v0.1.md` (its Global Constraints still hold).

## Global Constraints

- Everything in v0.1's Global Constraints, and:
- Weights: snow 0.41, cold 0.35, pressure 0.29, summer 0.24 (`score.WEIGHTS`, unchanged).
- `snow` = the maximum, over Nov Y … Mar Y+1, of the soum's monthly mean GLDAS `SnowDepth_inst` (m). The z is taken against the soum's winters 2001/02 … 2020/21. Deeper means a higher z.
- `summer` = the mean of the soum's July and August NDVI in year Y. The z is taken against summers 2001 … 2020. Lower NDVI means a higher z (sign flipped).
- A month counts only if its last day ≤ `as_of`. Baseline winters use `shift_as_of(as_of, y − year)` (v0.1 fix). An indicator with any of its months missing is `None`.
- **Hindcast winters:**
  - Dzud winters: 2009/10, 2015/16, 2022/23 and 2023/24.
  - Normal winters: the two lowest national-mortality non-dzud winters from 2011/12 to 2023/24, chosen by `normal_winters()`. Measured 2026-11-08 they are **2013/14 (0.94 %)** and **2021/22 (0.97 %)**.
- **Validation unit:**
  - Soum-level mortality exists only where NSO has soum losses for Y+1. 2010 has none, so 2009/10 is validated at aimag level only and drops out of the soum-level pooled ρ.
  - Aimag mortality always comes from NSO's aimag rows, in every winter. One source for every winter.
- Excess mortality = raw soum mortality minus the mean of that soum's raw mortality in the two normal winters.
- Credentials: read only from `~/.netrc`. Never logged, printed or written to `data/`.

## Data endpoints (checked live 2026-11-08 with the user's Earthdata login)

All endpoints use the same OPeNDAP pattern.
- **Request:** `GET <granule>.dap.nc4?dap4.ce=<path>[r0:r1][c0:c1]`.
- **Auth:** follow redirects through `urs.earthdata.nasa.gov` with Basic auth from `~/.netrc`, keeping cookies across the redirect.
- **Response:** NetCDF4 (HDF5) that `netCDF4.Dataset(path)` opens.

**GLDAS-2.1 Noah monthly** (`GLDAS_NOAH025_M` v2.1, collection `C1342986036-GES_DISC`, available up to 2026-07):
- Granule URL: `https://opendap.earthdata.nasa.gov/collections/C1342986036-GES_DISC/granules/GLDAS_NOAH025_M.2.1:GLDAS_NOAH025_M.A{YYYYMM}.021.nc4`.
- Constraint: `/SnowDepth_inst[0][406:448][1068:1199];/lat[406:448];/lon[1068:1199]`. That is 43 × 132 cells, lat 41.625 … 52.125 and lon 87.125 … 119.875, 0.25°.
- About 48 KB and 5 s per month.
- Ocean and water cells come back masked (`np.ma`). Treat them as no cell.
- Dec 2023 check: mean 0.128 m, max 0.69 m.

**MODIS MOD13C2 v061 monthly CMG NDVI**, 0.05° (collection `C2565788914-LPCLOUD`):
- Granule names carry a production stamp, so look each one up in CMR: `https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/granules.json?short_name=MOD13C2&version=061&temporal={YYYY}-{MM}-01T00:00:00Z,{YYYY}-{MM}-28T23:59:59Z&page_size=1`. Take the link starting `https://opendap.earthdata.nasa.gov/`.
- Constraint: `/MOD_Grid_monthly_CMG_VI/Data_Fields/CMG_0_05_Deg_Monthly_NDVI[755:970][5340:6000]`, which is 216 × 661 cells.
- Cell centres: row i → lat `90 − 0.05·(i + 0.5)`; column j → lon `−180 + 0.05·(j + 0.5)`.
- About 330 KB and 5 s per month.
- **Read raw:** call `set_auto_maskandscale(False)`, treat `-3000` as fill, and NDVI = raw / 10000. The file's `scale_factor` attribute is 10000 under the HDF-EOS convention (divide). netCDF4's automatic scaling would multiply.
- July 2023 mean over the box: 0.386.

**Builder's call recorded here:** `MOD13C2` (0.05° monthly) replaces the spec's `MOD13A2` (1 km 16-day).
- It is the same MODIS VI algorithm, aggregated.
- A soum averages about 190 cells.
- It is synchronous OPeNDAP rather than AppEEARS's queued jobs.

**NSO aimag rows:**
- The same two tables and POST body as v0.1, with the 21 three-digit aimag codes. UB's `511` is dropped.
- The join uses `norm(aimag name)` against COD `aimag_mn`.

## File structure

```
pipeline/http.py          + get_bytes_auth(url) -> bytes: urllib opener with HTTPCookieProcessor + Basic auth from ~/.netrc on urs.earthdata.nasa.gov; same retries; + TimeoutError retried (v0.1 deferred minor)
pipeline/power.py         cells_for vectorised with shapely.contains_xy (same contract, same tests)
pipeline/gldas.py         fetch Nov-Mar 2001-11 .. 2024-03 -> data/raw/gldas/*.nc4; soum monthly mean -> data/derived/soum_snow.csv
pipeline/ndvi.py          fetch Jul, Aug 2001..2023 -> data/raw/ndvi/*.nc4; soum monthly mean -> data/derived/soum_ndvi.csv
pipeline/nso.py           + aimag rows -> data/derived/livestock_aimag.csv; crosswalk asserts unique pcodes (v0.1 deferred minor)
pipeline/indicators.py    + snow(), summer(); scores all four; writes indicators_<year>.csv with z_snow, z_summer, snow_m, ndvi, n_indicators
pipeline/validate.py      aimag mortality from livestock_aimag.csv; soum mortality None-safe for years without soum losses; + excess mortality
pipeline/hindcast.py      WINTERS, normal_winters(), runs indicators/validate/export per winter, writes pooled.json
pipeline/export.py        + new props; writes site/data/winters.json (list of labels)
site/app.js, index.html   winter selector; popup shows all four z and which are missing; non-UB unscored labelled "insufficient data"; pooled panel
```

## Review Focus

1. **A GLDAS cell masked as water** (Khövsgöl lake, Uvs lake) is not counted as 0 m of snow. Covered by a `gldas` check.
2. **NDVI fill `-3000`, and the scale direction:** a raw 3861 must become 0.3861, not 38,610,000. Covered by an `ndvi` check.
3. **A soum with no soum-level losses for Y+1** (all of 2010): soum ρ is `null`, the aimag ρ is still computed, and nothing crashes. Covered by a `validate` check.
4. **A soum missing one indicator in a winter** (some soums have no 2009 census: 328 of 339 have one in 2010, which includes UB): it is scored on the indicators it has, `n_indicators` says how many, and the popup names the missing ones. Covered by an `indicators` check and by browser verification.
5. **An Earthdata auth failure** (expired or wrong password, an unapproved app): it raises a clear error naming `~/.netrc` and the host, never an HTML login page saved as `.nc4`. Covered by an `http` check against a fake opener.
