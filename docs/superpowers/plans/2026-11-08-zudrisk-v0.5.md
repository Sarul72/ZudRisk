## Agreed (play-back confirmed by the user)

**What "future risk" means.** It is structural vulnerability, not a forecast. The model learns from the history of soum-winters which soums lose heavily. It then replays the last 20 winters' weather on today's herds and pasture. The result is shown as "Эмзэг байдал" and labelled research, not a forecast.

**Model inputs and outputs.**
- **Training data:** every winter with NSO soum losses, 2011/12 to 2024/25. That is 14 winters × ~330 soums.
- **Features:**
  - the four indicator z-scores;
  - snow density z;
  - the NDVI trend up to that summer (a desertification proxy);
  - herd growth over 5 years;
  - the soum's mean mortality over *earlier* winters only.
- **Target:** a high-loss winter. Ruling, fixed before fitting: mortality ≥ 5 % of sheep units.
- **Model:** ridge logistic regression in numpy, with no new dependency.
- **Validation:** leave one winter out, scored against the fixed-weight score with Spearman ρ and AUC. The fitted weights are shown beside the fixed ones.

**Greenness trend.** The Theil–Sen slope of July–August NDVI from 2001 to the latest summer, per decade. It is a map layer, labelled a desertification proxy and not the official map.

**Map.**
- Layer choice: risk / vulnerability / greenness trend.
- Base map choice: OSM / OpenTopoMap.
- Soum search.
- Soum names on hover.

**Charts.** All are hand-made SVG, with no new dependency.
- In each soum and aimag popup: risk and recorded mortality across all winters.
- In the panel: national mortality by winter, skill by stage, and fixed vs model ρ.

**Ulaanbaatar.**
- The 6 districts with herds are scored. Their pressure uses Ulaanbaatar's density, the same way aimag centres use their aimag's.
- The 3 districts with no herds stay unscored, labelled "no registered herd". The rule is: no herd, no risk.

## Global constraints

- The v0.1–v0.4 constraints still hold.
- **No look-ahead in a training row.** Every feature of winter Y uses only data published by 31 March Y+1. The NDVI trend runs to summer Y. Past mortality covers winters before Y only.
- **The fixed weights are not tuned.** The model is reported beside them.
- **Every new check fails before its code exists.**
