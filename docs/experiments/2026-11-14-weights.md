# Indicator weights: pre-registration (2026-11-14)

Written and committed before any of the weight sets below is computed. The results go in a later commit, under "Results".

## Question

The score weights (snow 0.41, cold 0.35, pressure 0.29, summer 0.24) come from Nandintsetseg et al. (2018, Sci. Rep.
8:14769), fixed before any validation. Could weights estimated from ZudRisk's own data do better on winters they have not
seen? The forecast-combination literature warns that estimated weights often lose to fixed or equal weights out of sample
(Clemen 1989; Diebold & Shin 2019), so every candidate is judged only on held-out winters.

## Candidates (the four published indicators; no new terms; E1 is a separate decision)

- **W0 literature:** the published weights (baseline).
- **W1 equal:** 0.25 each.
- **W2 fitted:** non-negative weights summing to 1 on a grid of step 0.05, chosen to maximise the pooled Spearman ρ on the
  training winters.
- **W3 shrunk:** 0.5 × W0 + 0.5 × W2. The half is fixed now, not tuned.

## Two held-out tests

- **T1, leave one winter out:** for each of the 14 winters 2011/12–2024/25, W2 and W3 are fitted on the other 13 and score
  the held-out winter. The held-out scores are pooled into one ρ over every soum-winter (n = 4,688). The same ρ over the
  pre-registered winters (2013, 2015, 2021, 2022, 2023) is reported too.
- **T2, time split:** W2 and W3 are fitted on 2011/12–2017/18 and tested on 2018/19–2024/25. ρ is computed over the test
  soum-winters for W0, W1, W2 and W3.

## Decision rule

A candidate replaces the literature weights only if it beats W0 by **at least 0.01** in **both** T1 (every soum-winter)
and T2. The margin is 0.01 because E1's bootstrap showed differences near ±0.005 are within noise. Otherwise the literature
weights stay and the result is recorded. A replacement reaches the site only after the owner agrees, after 2026-11-15.
